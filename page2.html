How the Reality Fracture Deck Works

The primary strategy of the Reality Fracture deck is a "Polymorph" engine. You produce low-cost disposable tokens and transform them into game ending creature threats directly from your library.

Gameplan

  1. Make small tokens early to set up cheap token generators on turns 1–3 to create disposable token creatures.
  2. Cast a card that targets one of your tokens that sacrifices it to reveal cards from your library until you hit a creature.
  3. Because the deck only runs powerful expensive mana creature spells. This will garentee you will always hit a powerful creature spell.

Key Synergies

1. Token Generators

These spells and permanents make the expendable tokens you need to sacrifice:

  • Skrelv's Hive and Dreadhorde Invasion: Create free tokens every turn at low costs.
  • Secure the Wastes and Grand Crescendo: Create instant speed army of tokens to setup a big turn.
  • Shark Typhoon: Spawns flying Shark tokens whenever you cast noncreature spells.

2. Swap Engines

These spells sacrifice your small tokens into giant threats:(ps when I say "token at the bottom of your library" token can't be in the library so they cease to exist)

  • Proteus Staff: An artifact that lets you repeatedly put a token at the bottom of your library to get a the next creature straight from your deck onto the battlefield every turn.
  • Mass Polymorph and Synthetic Destiny: Exile your creature spells that turns your multiple small tokens creature into an entire army of giant creatures all at once.

3. High Impact Payoffs

When you sacrifice your token, you bypass high mana costs to pull cards like:

  • Archon of Cruelty: Forces opponents to sacrifice creatures, discard, and lose life while drawing you extra cards.
  • Serra's Emissary: Grants you and your creatures protection from a card type of your choice.
  • Archfiend of Despair: Prevents opponents from gaining life and doubles all damage dealt to them at the end of the turn.
